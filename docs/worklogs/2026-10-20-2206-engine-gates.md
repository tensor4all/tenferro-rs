# Engine-model gates and design records (#2206)

## Decisions

- Retargeted the session-entry audit to the surviving execution-engine entry
  surfaces instead of deleting it. The session mechanisms no longer exist, but
  the engine model still has reviewed boundaries: `impl Engine for T`,
  `impl ExecutionEngine for T`, `CudaEngine::open` and `with_raw`. The audit and
  allowlist were renamed to `scripts/audit-engine-entry.py` and
  `scripts/engine-entry-allowlist.json`; the negative self-tests seed each
  mechanism and the `cfg(test)`/alias cases.
- Replaced the `REPOSITORY_RULES.md` ownership contract value
  `execution-entry = CpuBackend` with `CpuEngine` and the resource name
  `CpuContext-Rayon` with `engine-owned-Rayon`, together with the source-contract
  assertion in `crates/tenferro-cpu/tests/integration/backend_capability_contracts.rs`.
- Retired the held-session, phase-lease, child-ticket and shared-scope design
  notes and recorded the engine/prepare/run model once in
  `docs/design/execution-engine-ownership.md`, which links the cross-repository
  decision in tensor4all-meta. Historical work logs and plans keep their original
  links; active design notes and guides were repointed.
- The session-route benchmark campaign was updated to the arm names the benches
  now emit: CPU `engine/single`/`engine/marginal16`, one `one_session` chain arm
  per shape, `prepared_graph` only, and the GPU `session/*` names the GPU bench
  still emits. The matched baseline carries the renamed CPU keys; its numbers
  remain the #1926 recapture until a fresh post-#2206 capture.

## Verification conclusions and constraints

- The engine audit passes on the tree and fails on each seeded violation.
- The session-route baseline and comparison scripts parse the renamed baseline;
  the committed matched baseline is partial for the GPU arms added after the
  recapture (`dot_general_into_*`, `prepared_*`). A comparison reports them as
  NEW until the next capture, and the capture expectation now counts the full
  current harness so that capture is the step that closes the gap.
- The `scripts/ci/tests` locale-sensitive archive-transfer assertion fails on
  this host because the tool prints Japanese `失敗`; the failure is unrelated to
  this change.
